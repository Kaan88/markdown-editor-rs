mdedit 1.0.1: select and copy text on the preview pane.

## New

- **Select text on the preview pane.** Drag over the page to select; double-click takes a word, triple-click a paragraph, and dragging past the pane's edge scrolls it. After a click on the page, Ctrl+C copies from it and Ctrl+A selects the whole page. Copy takes the rendered text, as mdview does, with formatting beside it for apps that take it. Escape, typing, or a click in the text gives the keyboard back to the text.
