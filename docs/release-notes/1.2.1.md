mdedit 1.2.1: an option to leave out tabs you barely looked at, and a tidier AI menu.

## New

- **Don't reopen tabs barely looked at.** A new setting in Settings > Session: at start, tabs you looked at for less than a number of seconds you choose (10 by default) are not opened again. Only the time a tab is in front while mdedit has the focus counts, added up across runs; switching tabs, switching to another program or minimizing stops the count. The tab you left on, pinned tabs and tabs with unsaved text always come back. Off by default.

## Fixed

- In the AI menu, a long saved prompt's name no longer runs under its Modify button; the menu keeps its width.
