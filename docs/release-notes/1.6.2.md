mdedit 1.6.2: text boxes that take the whole mouse, and drags that end when the window loses the focus.

This release moves mdedit onto the latest shared toolkit (`markdown-shared-rs` `84c0fc8`), whose
text boxes now handle the mouse as a whole.

## Text boxes

- **The command palette's box and a name being typed on the plan screen** take a double click (a
  word), a triple click (all of it), Shift and a click to extend, and a drag past either end,
  which scrolls the text on. A right click or Shift+F10 (on the plan screen also the Menu key)
  opens their edit menu: Cut, Copy, Paste, Select all.
- **The Find, Replace and Go to boxes, the results panel's boxes and the notes pane's name box**
  scroll their text on when a selection is dragged past either end, and take a click in their
  border or on the magnifier at the nearest end of the text.

## Fixes

- **Switching away during a drag** (Alt+Tab, or a dialog opening) no longer leaves the drag held:
  the next move after coming back no longer drags. A pane or panel keeps the size it was dragged
  to, a note being dragged in the notes pane goes back where it was, and a button, row or
  category held down is not clicked. This covers the text, the files pane and the results panel,
  the preview, the notes pane, the AI cards, the command palette, the plan screen and Settings.
