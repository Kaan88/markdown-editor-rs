mdedit 1.1.2: fixes from the sixth audit round of the unsaved-text recovery.

## Fixed

- **A reloaded tab stays editable.** Reloading a tab whose text was waiting for its recovery copy kept it read-only, so the copy was dropped at the next look. A reload that puts the text back now leaves the tab editable; it is read-only again only if the text still cannot be read.
- **No busy loop after a failed session write.** When the list of open tabs could not be saved and a retry was due, the window could keep waking up without writing anything. A due retry is now made even when nothing has changed: it succeeds and clears, or fails and is due again in 5 seconds.
